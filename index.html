08 / Questions

Common questions.

A few things businesses often want to know before starting a conversation.

V Hara provides business and legal consulting support and connects clients with suitable professionals for requirements such as business management, legal compliance, audit support and tax filing.

V Hara works through a partner-based model. Depending on the requirement, a suitable specialist may be connected with the client for the professional service itself, while V Hara supports coordination.

V Hara’s model is based on a curated professional network and matching the client’s requirement with relevant expertise. Specific engagement terms are discussed according to the requirement.

Fees depend on the service and professional engagement. Any applicable commercial terms should be clarified with V Hara before proceeding.
